## 💡 The Problem & The Solution

- **The Problem:** Daily standups, cross-functional engineering syncs, and sprint planning sessions often suffer from disengagement, awkward silences deciding who speaks next, and meeting fatigue.
- **The Solution:** A rapid, 90-second orbital flight challenge. Team members dodge hazards, harvest stardust, and outmaneuver rivals. When the flight clock strikes zero, the stardust scoreboard automatically dictates the daily speaking sequence—pairing accountability with team momentum.

---

## ✨ Core Product Features

- **⏱️ 90-Second Mission Clock:** Calibrated strictly for agile ceremonies—energizes teams without consuming valuable working meeting time.
- **👥 Dynamic Roster Engine:**
  - Real-time crew customization with zero-flicker pilot chips.
  - Inline click-to-edit name modification for all attendees.
  - Automated deep-linking: Room codes and customized rosters encode cleanly into shareable invite URLs (`?room=XYZ&crew=Name1,Name2`).
- **⚔️ Orbital Combat Mechanics:**
  - **Stardust Crystals:** Gather high-density crystals to expand ship mass and climb the radar tracker.
  - **Tactical Absorption:** Lock onto smaller rivals with dynamic green targeting reticles to absorb orbital mass.
  - **Singularity Avoidance:** Steer clear of crimson black holes that inflict hull decay.
  - **Anti-Camping Protocol:** Stationary vessels passively decay to enforce active navigation.
- **📋 1-Click Executive Export:** Formats today's complete turn order, player performance scores, monthly bounty, and icebreaker challenge prompt into clipboard-ready markdown for Slack and Microsoft Teams.
- **🎵 Retro Audio Architecture:** Procedurally generated 8-bit synth soundscapes, engine thrusters, and fanfare powered by the native Web Audio API (zero external runtime weight).

## 🚀 Facilitator Quick-Start

1. Open **[Cosmic Sync](https://soniran635.github.io/cosmic-sync-royale/)**.
2. Enter your **Commander Call-Sign** and **Mission Room Code** (e.g., `ENGINEERING-SYNC`).
3. Add your meeting attendees to the **Crew in Orbit** roster.
4. Click **🔗 Copy Link** and paste it into your meeting chat or calendar invite.
5. Hit **🚀 Launch into Orbit!** and let the leaderboard set your agenda!
